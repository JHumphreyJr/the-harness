A collaborative cooking app, delivered as a native iOS app (Swift) plus a website.

A recipe can be split up and shared among several people who are cooking it together. Each
person sees the steps that are theirs. Steps can be divided among the cooks before cooking
starts, or left unassigned and picked up by whoever is free as the cooking goes along.
Everyone can see overall progress: what is done, what is in progress and who has it, and what
is still open.

The iOS app is what people use while cooking. The website is for setting up a recipe and a
cooking session beforehand and for following along on a larger screen.
