### US state privacy control pack (CCPA / CPRA and similar)

Applies to this project.

- **Notice at collection** stating the categories of personal information, the purposes, and retention.
- **Consumer rights as features:** know and access, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, and limit the use of sensitive personal information, with verified requests fulfilled within the statutory time.
- **Honour opt-out preference signals** such as Global Privacy Control.
- **No sale or sharing** of personal information without the required opt-out link, and none for known minors without opt-in.
- **Service provider contracts** restricting each vendor's use of the data.
- **Reasonable security** proportionate to the data; retention limited to what the disclosed purposes need.
- **Right tools:** a request-handling workflow with identity verification and deadlines tracked, and detection of the browser's opt-out signal on the server side.
