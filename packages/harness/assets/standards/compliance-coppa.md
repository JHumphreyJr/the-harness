### Children's data control pack (COPPA and children's design codes)

Applies to this project because children may use it.

- **Verifiable parental consent** before collecting personal information from a child under 13, using a recognised method.
- **Collect the minimum** needed for the activity; no behavioural advertising; no making participation conditional on more data.
- **Clear notice** to parents of what is collected and how it is used, and a way for a parent to review, delete, and stop further collection.
- **Retention only as long as needed**, then secure deletion.
- **High privacy by default**, geolocation off, no nudges toward weaker settings, age-appropriate language.
- **Third parties** that receive children's data are listed and bound to the same limits.
- **Right tools:** an established age-assurance and parental-consent provider, not a self-declared birthdate field alone.
