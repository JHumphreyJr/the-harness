### EU Cyber Resilience Act control pack

Applies to this project because it is a product with digital elements made available on the EU market.

- **Secure by design and by default:** no known exploitable vulnerabilities at release, secure default configuration, protection of confidentiality and integrity, minimal attack surface.
- **Software bill of materials** in a commonly used, machine-readable format covering at least top-level dependencies, kept for every released version. The harness produces this.
- **Vulnerability handling for the whole support period:** a published contact and coordinated disclosure policy, a process to triage, fix and release security updates without delay, and security updates delivered separately from feature updates where feasible.
- **A stated support period** and a commitment to security updates during it.
- **Reporting:** actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents are reported to the designated CSIRT and ENISA within the deadlines of Article 14. Know who in the organisation does this.
- **Technical documentation and risk assessment** kept current and retained for the period the regulation sets; a conformity assessment by the route the product's class requires.
- **Right tools:** an SBOM generator in CI, continuous dependency vulnerability monitoring against the SBOM, VEX or CSAF for advisories, and signed releases.
